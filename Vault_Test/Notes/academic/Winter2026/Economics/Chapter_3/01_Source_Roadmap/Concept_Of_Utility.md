## Mental Model

The concept of utility is a fundamental idea in economics that describes the level of happiness, pleasure, or satisfaction a person gets from consuming a good or service. It's a way to measure preferences and assign a numerical value to the satisfaction someone receives from a particular bundle of goods.

## The Economic Intuition

Utility is a subjective measure that varies from person to person, place to place, and time to time. It's essential to understand that utility and usefulness are not synonymous. Utility is a way to describe how much satisfaction someone gets from consuming something, while usefulness refers to the ability of something to serve a purpose.

The concept of utility assumes that satisfaction can be measured in units, just like the actual goods consumed. A utility function is a way to assign a number to a consumption bundle such that more preferred bundles get assigned larger numbers than less-preferred bundles.

## The Choice Mechanism

When faced with two consumption bundles, X and Y, a consumer will prefer X over Y if and only if the utility of X is greater than the utility of Y. This is a crucial aspect of utility, as it helps us understand how consumers make choices.

| Bundle | Utility |
| --- | --- |
| X | 10 |
| Y | 8 |

In this example, the consumer prefers bundle X over bundle Y because it has a higher utility.
