## Mental Model
The concept of Total Utility is essential in understanding consumer behavior in economics. It refers to the overall satisfaction or pleasure a consumer derives from consuming a particular good or service.

## The Economic Intuition
The Total Utility curve has a specific shape. It first increases as the quantity consumed increases, reaches its maximum, and then declines. This happens because as a consumer consumes more units of a good, the marginal satisfaction they derive from each additional unit eventually decreases.

## The Choice Mechanism
Consumers make choices based on their preferences and the utility they derive from different goods and services. The Total Utility concept helps explain why consumers tend to consume more of a good when its price is lower and less when its price is higher.
