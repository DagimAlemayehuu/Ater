The theory of consumer behavior evolution explains how consumers make decisions about what goods and services to purchase. It is based on the concept of utility, which refers to the satisfaction or pleasure a consumer derives from consuming a product.

## The Economic Intuition
The economic intuition behind the theory of consumer behavior evolution is that consumers try to maximize their utility given their limited resources. They make choices about how to allocate their income among different goods and services to achieve the highest level of satisfaction. The concept of utility is crucial in understanding consumer behavior, as it helps economists predict how consumers will respond to changes in prices, income, and other factors.

The cardinal utility theory assumes that utility is measurable and can be expressed in units called utils. This theory also assumes that the utility derived from one unit of a commodity is equal to the amount of money a consumer is willing to pay for it.
