## Mental Model
The theory of consumer behavior is a fundamental concept in microeconomics that explains how consumers make decisions about what goods and services to buy. It is based on the idea that consumers have limited resources, such as income, and must make choices about how to allocate those resources to maximize their satisfaction.

## The Economic Intuition
The economic intuition behind the theory of consumer behavior is that consumers will make choices that maximize their utility, or satisfaction, given their limited resources. This means that consumers will weigh the costs and benefits of different goods and services and choose the ones that provide the most satisfaction. The theory assumes that consumers are rational and have complete information about the market.

## The Choice Mechanism
The choice mechanism is the process by which consumers make decisions about what goods and services to buy. It involves identifying the consumer's preferences, income, and the prices of different goods and services. The consumer will then choose the combination of goods and services that maximizes their utility, subject to their budget constraint.

| --------- | ----------- |
| Preferences | The likes and dislikes of a consumer |
| Budget Constraint | The limited income of a consumer |

## The Formal Math & Models
The formal math and models used in the theory of consumer behavior include the budget constraint, indifference curves, and the utility function. These models help to explain how consumers make choices and how changes in prices and income affect those choices.
