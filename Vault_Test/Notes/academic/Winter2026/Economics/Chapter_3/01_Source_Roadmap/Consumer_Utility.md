## Mental Model
The concept of utility is a measure of the level of happiness, pleasure, or satisfaction that someone receives from consuming a good or service. It's a way to describe preferences and assign numbers to consumption bundles such that more preferred bundles get assigned larger numbers than less-preferred bundles.

## The Economic Intuition
Utility is a subjective measure that can vary from person to person, place to place, and time to time. It's essential to understand that utility and usefulness are not synonymous. Utility assumes that satisfaction can be measured in units, similar to how goods consumed can be determined. Given any two consumption bundles X and Y, a consumer prefers X over Y if and only if the utility of X is better than the utility of Y.

## The Choice Mechanism
This concept helps us understand how consumers make choices based on their preferences. A utility function is a way of assigning a number to a consumption bundle such that more preferred bundles get assigned larger numbers than less-preferred bundles.

## The Formal Math & Models
Utility functions are used to assign numbers to consumption bundles.
