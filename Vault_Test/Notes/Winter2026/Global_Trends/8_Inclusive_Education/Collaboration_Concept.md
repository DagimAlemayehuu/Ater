## Mental Model

Imagine a big ****puzzle**** with many **different** **shaped** pieces. Each puzzle piece **represents** a **person** with their own **unique** skills and abilities. When people work together, they fit their puzzle pieces together to create a complete picture, which is the project or goal they're trying to achieve. Just like how a puzzle piece might not fit with one other piece, but fits perfectly with another, people with different abilities can work together to create something amazing.

## How It Works

Collaboration is when people work together to create or produce something, using their individual skills and abilities. It exists because when people with different strengths and talents work together, they can achieve more than they could alone. The way it operates is that each person brings their own special qualities to the project, and together, they find a way to make it work, without judging or discriminating against others based on their abilities. This way, everyone can contribute and be a valuable part of the team.

This concept is fundamentally connected to [[Characteristics_Of_Successful_Partnerships]], [[Collaboration_And_Partnership]], and operates within the [[Community_Involvement_Strategies]] framework.

## Key Details

Collaboration is formally defined as the act of working together to produce or create something according to the capacities and abilities of individuals. This concept is fundamental in special needs education, emphasizing the importance of teamwork and [[Stakeholder_Partnerships]]. The core idea of collaboration revolves around leveraging individual strengths and qualities to achieve a common goal. It inherently implies a non-discriminatory approach, where individuals are valued for their unique contributions regardless of their abilities in comparison to others.

- **Lack of Individual Contribution**: When individuals are not valued for their unique qualities, their contributions to the collaborative effort may be diminished or overlooked.
- **Mismatched Abilities**: A mismatch in abilities among team members can lead to challenges in collaboration if not managed properly, potentially hindering the achievement of common goals.
- **Discriminatory Practices**: Discriminatory practices within a collaborative setting can undermine the principles of collaboration, creating an unequal or unfair working environment.
