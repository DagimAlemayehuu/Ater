## THE CHALLENGE

### Q1 | MCQ

A consortium of independent scholars is established to conduct a 'Fiscal Policy Research' project. The group has a formal written agreement outlining specific roles and responsibilities, and they have ensured that membership is open to all qualified scholars regardless of their institutional affiliation. However, the consortium decides that the resulting research grants and publication credits will be allocated based on the prestige of the scholar's home institution rather than being shared equitably. According to the characteristics of a successful partnership, which core element is being undermined?

- [ ] **A)** The existence of a clear agreement among stakeholders
- [ ] **B)** The principle of membership without discrimination
- [ ] **C)** The establishment of an equal and equitable relationship through shared benefits
- [ ] **D)** The transparency regarding the nature of liability


---

## SOLUTION KEY

<details>
<summary>Click to reveal answers</summary>

#### Q1

- **Answer:** `C`
- **Explanation:** A successful partnership requires that benefits are shared fairly and that an equal and equitable relationship is established. By allocating grants and credits based on institutional prestige rather than equitably, the consortium creates an unequal distribution of benefits, which the source explicitly states can lead to resentment and dissatisfaction, thereby undermining the partnership's foundation.
