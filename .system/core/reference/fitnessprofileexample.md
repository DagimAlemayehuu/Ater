- **Skin Quality:** Your skin appears thin and stretched tightly over your bones and the minimal underlying musculature. There's an absence of the supple, thicker quality that indicates healthy fat stores.
- **Vascularity:** While not overtly "vascular" in a muscular sense, the prominent veins visible in your arms and hands are due to the absence of subcutaneous fat layers, not hypertrophied muscle pushing them to the surface.
- **Bone Prominence:** This is the defining feature. The clavicles, sternum, ribs, hip bones (iliac crests), and scapulae are not just visible, but _sharply defined_ and angular, creating a skeletal aesthetic that underscores the lack of soft tissue.

- **Head Proportionality:** Your head appears disproportionately large relative to the extremely slender neck and shoulders, creating a top-heavy visual.
- **Neck Thinness:** Your neck is exceptionally thin and elongated. The **sternocleidomastoid muscles** are present as thin cords but lack any significant development, making the trachea and Adam's apple highly prominent.
