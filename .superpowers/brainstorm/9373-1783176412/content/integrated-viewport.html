Visual Ingestion & Note Browsing

Instead of the `/database` folder tree, clicking a course card (e.g. OOP WITH JAVA) or a Study Hub transitions the main viewport to show a simplified, graphical view of notes, reference PDFs, and the ingestion status for that specific subject. Notes are opened in a full-screen focus reader.

2

Active Recall Engine Integration

The FSRS practice schedule is bound directly to the Weekly Study Calendar card. If you have cards due, clicking "Practice Due" launches the practice session in place, completely replacing the need for a separate top-level Practice Hub page.

3

NotebookLM Podcast & Studio Bar

Generated audio summaries, studio assets, and Google notebooks are listed as widgets on the right column or under their corresponding Course panels, rather than living on a disconnected page.
