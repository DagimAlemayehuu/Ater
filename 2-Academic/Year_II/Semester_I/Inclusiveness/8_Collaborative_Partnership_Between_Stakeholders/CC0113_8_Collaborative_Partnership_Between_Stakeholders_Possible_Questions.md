# Part II: Unit Synthesis (The Final Boss)

### Integrated Scenario: Designing an Inclusive Community Center
**The Setup:** You are part of a steering committee tasked with designing a new, fully inclusive community center. This project involves input from various **stakeholders** including local government, disability advocacy groups, youth organizations, senior citizens, and potential donors. The goal is to ensure the center meets diverse needs and fosters a strong sense of **collaboration** and **partnership** among its users and operators.
**The Constraints:** You have a limited budget, a tight construction timeline, and conflicting design preferences from different stakeholder groups.
**The Challenge:**
(a) Identify at least five distinct stakeholders for this project and describe the unique "stake" each has.
(b) Propose a **collaborative partnership** strategy that addresses the conflicting design preferences while adhering to the budget and timeline. Your strategy should leverage key elements of successful collaboration.
(c) Predict a potential "challenge to collaboration" that might arise during the project's execution and outline a proactive strategy to mitigate it, referencing the benefits of collaboration.
