## THE CHALLENGE

### Q1 | MCQ

What is the primary purpose of Critical Theories in the field of International Relations?

- [ ] **A)** To reinforce mainstream approaches such as liberalism and realism
- [ ] **B)** To provide a platform for marginalized voices and challenge dominant narratives
- [ ] **C)** To focus solely on economic factors influencing international relations
- [ ] **D)** To promote a single, universally accepted theory of international relations


---

### Q2 | MCQ

What does the term 'State System Definition' refer to in the context of international relations?

- [ ] **A)** The structure or context within which states cooperate, compete, and confront each other over issues of national interest
- [ ] **B)** The economic standing of a state in the global economy
- [ ] **C)** The geographical position of a state in the international system
- [ ] **D)** The historical ties and experiences of a state


---

### Q3 | MCQ

Which statement best matches the source's treatment of Democratic Peace Theory?

- [ ] **A)** The text explains the democratic peace theory as a development of Kant's ideas on liberal states and peace.
- [ ] **B)** Democratic Peace Theory is unrelated to the subject's behavior.
- [ ] **C)** Democratic Peace Theory only describes comments and formatting.
- [ ] **D)** Democratic Peace Theory can be ignored without changing program behavior.


---

## SOLUTION KEY

<details>
<summary>Click to reveal answers</summary>

#### Q1

- **Answer:** `B`
- **Explanation:** Critical Theories aim to challenge commonly held assumptions in International Relations and provide a voice to marginalized individuals, thereby offering alternative perspectives to mainstream approaches like liberalism and realism.

#### Q2

- **Answer:** `A`
- **Explanation:** The State System Definition refers to the structure or context within which states interact, which includes their cooperation, competition, and confrontation over national interest issues.

#### Q3

- **Answer:** `A`
- **Explanation:** The source context connects Democratic Peace Theory to concrete behavior, syntax, or logical structure from page 27.
