## THE CHALLENGE

### Q1 | MCQ

What is the primary focus of the concept of actors in international relations?

- [ ] **A)** Study of international law and diplomacy
- [ ] **B)** Entities interacting in the global arena
- [ ] **C)** Power relations and governance
- [ ] **D)** Structure of the international system


---

### Q2 | MCQ

Which of the following actors in international relations is an example of a non-state entity that can influence global politics?

- [ ] **A)** International Monetary Fund
- [ ] **B)** United Nations
- [ ] **C)** Multinational Corporation
- [ ] **D)** Non-Governmental Organization


---

### Q3 | MCQ

What is the primary purpose of Critical Theories in the field of International Relations?

- [ ] **A)** To reinforce mainstream approaches such as liberalism and realism
- [ ] **B)** To provide a platform for marginalized voices and challenge dominant narratives
- [ ] **C)** To focus solely on economic factors influencing international relations
- [ ] **D)** To promote a single, universally accepted theory of international relations


---

### Q4 | MCQ

What type of power distribution is characterized by several concentrations of power among states?

- [ ] **A)** Unipolarity
- [ ] **B)** Bipolarity
- [ ] **C)** Multipolarity
- [ ] **D)** Bilateral


---

### Q5 | MCQ

What is a key characteristic of the European model of statehood in the International Relations Model?

- [ ] **A)** Sovereign states with defined territories and fortified borders
- [ ] **B)** Tribal councils with fluid territories
- [ ] **C)** City-states with rotating leadership
- [ ] **D)** Global governance with no national borders


---

## SOLUTION KEY

<details>
<summary>Click to reveal answers</summary>

#### Q1

- **Answer:** `B`
- **Explanation:** N/A

#### Q2

- **Answer:** `C`
- **Explanation:** The correct answer is C, Multinational Corporation, as it is a non-state entity that can influence global politics through its economic activities and operations across borders. The other options are either state-led or intergovernmental organizations.

#### Q3

- **Answer:** `B`
- **Explanation:** Critical Theories aim to challenge commonly held assumptions in International Relations and provide a voice to marginalized individuals, thereby offering alternative perspectives to mainstream approaches like liberalism and realism.

#### Q4

- **Answer:** `C`
- **Explanation:** According to the concept of Power Distribution, multipolarity is characterized by several concentrations of power among states, as opposed to unipolarity, which has one main concentration, and bipolarity, which has two.

#### Q5

- **Answer:** `A`
- **Explanation:** The European model of statehood, which is central to the International Relations Model, emphasizes sovereign states with defined territories, fortified borders, capitals, armies, foreign ministries, flags, and national anthems.
