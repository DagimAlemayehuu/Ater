### Q2 | MCQ

What does the concept of State Sovereignty primarily refer to?

- [ ] **A)** The ability of a state to form alliances with other states
- [ ] **B)** The exclusive right of a state to rule its own territory and act as it sees fit in relation to other states
- [ ] **C)** The responsibility of a state to protect its citizens from external threats
- [ ] **D)** The economic power of a state in the global market

### Q3 | MCQ

Which of the following actors in international relations is an example of a non-state entity that can influence global politics?

- [ ] **A)** International Monetary Fund
- [ ] **B)** United Nations
- [ ] **C)** Multinational Corporation
- [ ] **D)** Non-Governmental Organization

### Q4 | MCQ

What is the primary purpose of Critical Theories in the field of International Relations?

- [ ] **A)** To reinforce mainstream approaches such as liberalism and realism
- [ ] **B)** To provide a platform for marginalized voices and challenge dominant narratives
- [ ] **C)** To focus solely on economic factors influencing international relations
- [ ] **D)** To promote a single, universally accepted theory of international relations

### Q5 | MCQ

What is the primary characteristic of an Anarchic System in the context of international relations?

- [ ] **A)** A centralized authority governing all states
- [ ] **B)** The presence of a hegemonic power
- [ ] **C)** The absence of a common sovereign and decentralized distribution of power
- [ ] **D)** A system of international law enforced by a global institution

## SOLUTION KEY

<details>
<summary>Click to reveal answers</summary>

#### Q1

- **Answer:** `A`
- **Explanation:** The source context connects Structuralism And Marxism to concrete behavior, syntax, or logical structure from page 36.

#### Q2

- **Answer:** `B`
- **Explanation:** State Sovereignty refers to the exclusive right of a state to rule its own territory and act as it sees fit in relation to other states. This concept was symbolized by the Treaty of Westphalia in 1648.

#### Q3

- **Answer:** `C`
- **Explanation:** The correct answer is C, Multinational Corporation, as it is a non-state entity that can influence global politics through its economic activities and operations across borders. The other options are either state-led or intergovernmental organizations.

#### Q4

- **Answer:** `B`
- **Explanation:** Critical Theories aim to challenge commonly held assumptions in International Relations and provide a voice to marginalized individuals, thereby offering alternative perspectives to mainstream approaches like liberalism and realism.

#### Q5

- **Answer:** `C`
- **Explanation:** An Anarchic System is defined by the lack of a common sovereign over all states and the decentralized distribution of power among them. This leads to states primarily acting in their own national interests without a higher authority to enforce common rules.
