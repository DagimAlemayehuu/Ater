### Q1 | MCQ

What is the primary implication of globalization on international relations?

- [ ] **A)** Reduced interdependence among nation-states
- [ ] **B)** Increased isolationism
- [ ] **C)** Enhanced global governance
- [ ] **D)** Deepened interconnectedness among people, groups, and nations


---

### Q2 | MCQ

What is the core idea of nationalism according to its definition?

- [ ] **A)** The doctrine that asserts the global community as the basic political unit
- [ ] **B)** The ideology that prioritizes regional autonomy over national unity
- [ ] **C)** The doctrine that asserts the nation as the basic political unit in organizing society
- [ ] **D)** The concept that advocates for the abolition of national borders


---

### Q3 | MCQ

Which statement best matches the source's treatment of Democratic Peace Theory?

- [ ] **A)** The text explains the democratic peace theory as a development of Kant's ideas on liberal states and peace.
- [ ] **B)** Democratic Peace Theory is unrelated to the subject's behavior.
- [ ] **C)** Democratic Peace Theory only describes comments and formatting.
- [ ] **D)** Democratic Peace Theory can be ignored without changing program behavior.


---

### Q4 | MCQ

Which of the following actors in international relations is an example of a non-state entity that can influence global politics?

- [ ] **A)** International Monetary Fund
- [ ] **B)** United Nations
- [ ] **C)** Multinational Corporation
- [ ] **D)** Non-Governmental Organization


---

### Q5 | MCQ

What is the primary focus of the field of study known as international relations?

- [ ] **A)** The internal politics of a single nation-state
- [ ] **B)** The interactions and transactions between nations, states, and other actors in the global arena
- [ ] **C)** The economic systems of various countries
- [ ] **D)** The history of a specific region


---

## SOLUTION KEY

<details>
<summary>Click to reveal answers</summary>

#### Q1

- **Answer:** `D`
- **Explanation:** Globalization refers to the complex and multifaceted interactions between people, groups, firms, associations, parties, nations, or states. It leads to a world where experiences and transactions are increasingly interconnected on an international dimension.

#### Q2

- **Answer:** `C`
- **Explanation:** The definition of nationalism provided emphasizes that it is the doctrine that asserts the nation as the basic political unit in organizing society. This directly aligns with option C, making it the correct answer.

#### Q3

- **Answer:** `A`
- **Explanation:** The source context connects Democratic Peace Theory to concrete behavior, syntax, or logical structure from page 27.

#### Q4

- **Answer:** `C`
- **Explanation:** The correct answer is C, Multinational Corporation, as it is a non-state entity that can influence global politics through its economic activities and operations across borders. The other options are either state-led or intergovernmental organizations.

#### Q5

- **Answer:** `B`
- **Explanation:** The field of international relations focuses on the interactions and transactions between nations, states, and other actors in the global arena, making option B the correct answer.
