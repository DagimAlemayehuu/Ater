### Q1 | MCQ

What is the primary characteristic of an Anarchic System in the context of international relations?

- [ ] **A)** A centralized authority governing all states
- [ ] **B)** The presence of a hegemonic power
- [ ] **C)** The absence of a common sovereign and decentralized distribution of power
- [ ] **D)** A system of international law enforced by a global institution


---

### Q2 | MCQ

Which statement best matches the source's treatment of Structuralism And Marxism?

- [ ] **A)** Structuralism and Marxism critique traditional theories for their obsession with the state and the status quo, highlight
- [ ] **B)** Structuralism And Marxism is unrelated to the subject's behavior.
- [ ] **C)** Structuralism And Marxism only describes comments and formatting.
- [ ] **D)** Structuralism And Marxism can be ignored without changing program behavior.


---

### Q3 | MCQ

According to Hans Morgenthau's realism theory in International Relations, what is the primary focus of international relations?

- [ ] **A)** Economic interdependence among nations
- [ ] **B)** Actors' power relations in the supra-national domain
- [ ] **C)** Cultural exchange and cooperation
- [ ] **D)** Environmental protection and sustainability


---

## SOLUTION KEY

<details>
<summary>Click to reveal answers</summary>

#### Q1

- **Answer:** `C`
- **Explanation:** An Anarchic System is defined by the lack of a common sovereign over all states and the decentralized distribution of power among them. This leads to states primarily acting in their own national interests without a higher authority to enforce common rules.

#### Q2

- **Answer:** `A`
- **Explanation:** The source context connects Structuralism And Marxism to concrete behavior, syntax, or logical structure from page 36.

#### Q3

- **Answer:** `B`
- **Explanation:** Morgenthau's realism theory posits that international relations is essentially about actors' power relations in the supra-national domain, shaping the structure of the international system.
