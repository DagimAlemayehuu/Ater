# 2. The Core Execution

### Global Strategy

**Individual Level Analysis** refers to an approach that examines the behaviors, motivations, beliefs, and orientation of the individual in affecting a particular international phenomenon. This level of analysis looks at the psychology and emotions behind people's actions and decisions, including their fears, visions, and access to information. By focusing on the individual level, researchers aim to understand how the psychological factors and personal characteristics of individuals influence their decisions and behavior in the context of international relations.

The underlying mechanism of **Individual Level Analysis** involves examining the psychological factors that drive individual decision-making. This includes considering the mindsets and perceptions of political leaders and key actors, and how these shape their choices and actions. By analyzing the individual level, researchers can gain insight into how specific personalities, with their unique experiences, biases, and motivations, contribute to international phenomena. This approach recognizes that individuals, rather than just states or groups, play a crucial role in shaping international outcomes.

The real-world significance of Individual Level Analysis lies in its ability to provide a nuanced understanding of the causes and consequences of international phenomena. By focusing on the actions and decisions of specific individuals, researchers can draw different conclusions about the underlying drivers of international events. For instance, the analysis of a particular politician's decision-making process can reveal the importance of psychological factors, such as personal relationships or emotional responses, in shaping their choices. This, in turn, can help policymakers and scholars better understand the complexities of international relations and develop more effective strategies for addressing global challenges.

### Diplomatic Friction

**Individual Level Analysis** is a methodological approach in International Relations that scrutinizes the psychological and emotional underpinnings of individual decision-making, examining how these factors influence actions and outcomes in the international arena. This approach focuses on the behaviors, motivations, and beliefs of individuals, recognizing that their unique perceptions and experiences play a crucial role in shaping international phenomena. By analyzing the individual level, researchers seek to understand the complex interplay between psychological factors and international outcomes, acknowledging that individual actors, with their distinct characteristics, contribute significantly to the dynamics of international relations. The individual level of analysis is distinguished by its attention to the personal characteristics of state actors and other individuals who influence international relations, and how these characteristics affect their decisions and actions. This approach complements other [[Levels_Of_Analysis]], such as the state and system levels, providing a more comprehensive understanding of international relations.
