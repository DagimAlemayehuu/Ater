## Mental Model

A coral reef's skeleton is its core structure, providing protection and sustenance for the entire ecosystem. Just as the reef's foundation and framework are vital for the survival of its inhabitants and the preservation of their habitat, a nation's **Core Interests and Values** serve as the essential, non-negotiable foundation for its self-preservation, safeguarding its systems, people, culture, and territory. If the reef's skeleton is compromised, the entire ecosystem suffers; similarly, if a nation's **Core Interests and Values** are threatened, its very existence and sovereignty are at risk.

## The Global Economic Engine

**Core interests and values** refer to goals for which people are willing to make sacrifices, related to self-preservation of a state's systems, people, culture, and territory. These are objectives that are considered vital to the existence and well-being of a nation, and are therefore pursued with great urgency and commitment.

The underlying mechanism of **core interests and values** lies in their relationship to the fundamental survival and security of a state. They are goals that are deemed essential to the preservation of a state's political and economic systems, its people, culture, and [[Territorial_Integrity]]. As such, they are typically prioritized above other objectives, and are often considered non-negotiable. The achievement of core interests and values is also seen as a prerequisite for the realization of other goals, as the existence and stability of the state are essential for the pursuit of any other objective.

The real-world significance of core interests and values lies in their ability to shape a nation's [[Foreign_Policy]] and international relations. They are often enshrined as basic principles of **foreign policy**, and become articles of faith that are accepted by society without question. The definition of core interests and values can vary from country to country, depending on the attitudes and priorities of those who make [[Foreign_Policy]]. However, their importance in guiding a nation's interactions with the international community cannot be overstated, as they are often seen as essential to the very survival and prosperity of the state.

## The Macro Model & Jargon

**Core interests and values** constitute paramount objectives that garner unwavering commitment and willingness to make sacrifices, underpinning the self-preservation of a nation's fundamental systems, populace, cultural fabric, and [[Territorial_Integrity]]. These objectives are prioritized due to their indispensability to the existence and well-being of a state, necessitating their realization as a prerequisite for pursuing other goals. The definition and prioritization of **core interests and values** are contingent upon the perspectives and attitudes of [[Foreign_Policy]] decision-makers. Consequently, these objectives are often codified as basic principles of **foreign policy**, enjoying unquestioned societal acceptance. The attainment of core interests and values ensures a state's sovereignty and independence, thereby providing the foundation for the pursuit of additional objectives.
