## Definition & Boundary Conditions

[[Community_Involvement]] is a cornerstone of inclusive development, predicated on the active participation of all individuals, irrespective of their diverse backgrounds. It encompasses the creation of opportunities that enable every community member to contribute to and influence the development process, ensuring equitable distribution of benefits. This approach is underpinned by a commitment to non-discriminatory communication and the recognition of diverse experiences. By fostering an environment of inclusivity, [[Community_Involvement]] seeks to promote equality and respect for all individuals, particularly in the realms of education and employability.
