# 2 Theory of Demand and Supply Hub

## Overview
The Basics of Economics Hub revolves around the fundamental concepts of demand and supply, which interlock to form the larger system of market economics. The theory of demand describes the relationship between consumer demand for goods and services and their prices, governed by the law of demand, which states that price and quantity demanded are inversely related. Demand can be represented through demand schedules, curves, and functions, and is influenced by various factors, including price, income, and prices of related goods. The degree of responsiveness of demand to these factors is measured through price elasticity, income elasticity, and cross-price elasticity of demand.

On the other hand, the theory of supply describes the relationship between producers' supply of goods and services and their prices, governed by the law of supply, which states that price and quantity supplied are directly related. Supply can be represented through supply schedules and curves, and is influenced by factors such as technological advancements, costs of production, and changes in the industry. The degree of responsiveness of supply to changes in price is measured through price elasticity of supply. The supply curve can shift due to various factors, including changes in production costs, industry size, and government policies.

The demand and supply curves intersect at the point of market equilibrium, where market demand equals market supply. Changes in demand and supply can disrupt this equilibrium, leading to changes in the equilibrium price level and quantity. Understanding these concepts, including elasticity, shifts in demand and supply curves, and market equilibrium, provides a foundation for analyzing and predicting market behavior, and making informed decisions in the field of economics.
