## 🎯 THE CHALLENGE

### Q1 | MCQ
In a developing country, if the government increases its spending on infrastructure, what happens to the aggregate demand and the country's economic growth rate?

- [ ] **A)** Aggregate demand increases, and the economic growth rate rises
- [ ] **B)** Aggregate demand decreases, and the economic growth rate falls

---


## 🔐 SOLUTION KEY (DO NOT PEEK)

<details>
<summary>Click to reveal answers and technical logic</summary>

#### Q1 Logic
- **Definitive Answer:** `A`
- **Mechanism:** When the government increases its spending on infrastructure, it directly adds to the aggregate demand. This increase in aggregate demand can stimulate economic growth as more resources are being utilized. Therefore, the economic growth rate is likely to rise as a result of increased government expenditure.
