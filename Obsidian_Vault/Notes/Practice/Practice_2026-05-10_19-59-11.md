## 🎯 THE CHALLENGE

### Q1 | DEBUG
In Fiscal Policy Research, analyze the impact of a sudden increase in government spending on the overall level of inflation in an economy operating at full capacity.

```text
An economist incorrectly assumes that an increase in government spending will immediately increase the money supply, causing inflation to rise. However, they fail to account for the effects of crowding out and the economy's current state of full capacity.
```

---


## 🔐 SOLUTION KEY (DO NOT PEEK)

<details>
<summary>Click to reveal answers and technical logic</summary>

#### Q1 Logic
- **Definitive Answer:** `The increase in government spending will lead to an increase in aggregate demand, but because the economy is at full capacity, this will primarily cause inflation to rise due to demand-pull inflation, not an increase in the money supply.`
- **Mechanism:** In an economy operating at full capacity, an increase in government spending increases aggregate demand. Since supply cannot increase beyond capacity, prices rise, causing demand-pull inflation. The initial assumption about the money supply increase is not the primary channel through which inflation rises in this scenario.
