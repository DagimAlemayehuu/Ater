## 🎯 THE CHALLENGE

### Q1 | SYNTHESIS
In a market strategy, if the government increases the tax on imported electronics, which leads to a rise in their prices, what happens to the demand for domestic electronics and the overall market equilibrium?


---


## 🔐 SOLUTION KEY (DO NOT PEEK)

<details>
<summary>Click to reveal answers and technical logic</summary>

#### Q1 Logic
- **Definitive Answer:** `The demand for domestic electronics increases, and the market equilibrium shifts towards a higher price and potentially higher quantity for domestic electronics, assuming they are substitutes.`
- **Mechanism:** When the government increases the tax on imported electronics, it makes them more expensive for consumers. This change in price can lead consumers to seek alternatives, such as domestic electronics, assuming they are substitutes. As demand for domestic electronics increases, their price may also rise. The overall market equilibrium will shift depending on the elasticity of demand and supply for domestic electronics. If domestic electronics are seen as close substitutes, their demand will increase more significantly, potentially leading to a higher market equilibrium price and possibly a higher quantity if supply can accommodate the increased demand.
