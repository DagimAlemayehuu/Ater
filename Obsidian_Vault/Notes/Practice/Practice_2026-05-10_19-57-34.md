## 🎯 THE CHALLENGE

### Q1 | WRITING
In a closed economy with a government sector, if the government increases its spending (G) by $100 million, and the marginal propensity to consume (MPC) is 0.8, what happens to the equilibrium level of national income (Y) in the short run, assuming no changes in taxes (T) or net exports (NX)?


---


## 🔐 SOLUTION KEY (DO NOT PEEK)

<details>
<summary>Click to reveal answers and technical logic</summary>

#### Q1 Logic
- **Definitive Answer:** `The equilibrium level of national income (Y) increases by $500 million.`
- **Mechanism:** When government spending (G) increases by $100 million, it directly increases aggregate demand. Given the MPC of 0.8, for every dollar increase in income, 80 cents is spent on consumption. The increase in G leads to a multiplier effect. The spending multiplier (k) is calculated as 1 / (1 - MPC) = 1 / (1 - 0.8) = 1 / 0.2 = 5. Therefore, a $100 million increase in G results in a $500 million increase in Y (100 million * 5).
