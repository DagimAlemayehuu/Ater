## THE CHALLENGE

### Q1 | MCQ
In a developing economy, what happens to the demand for domestic goods when the exchange rate depreciates, assuming all else remains constant?

- [ ] **A)** The demand for domestic goods decreases because imports become more expensive.
- [ ] **B)** The demand for domestic goods increases because domestic goods become cheaper for foreign buyers.
- [ ] **C)** The demand for domestic goods remains unchanged as exchange rates only affect services.
- [ ] **D)** The demand for domestic goods decreases because domestic consumers switch to imported goods.

---

### Q2 | TRUE FALSE
In a market economy, an increase in consumer income leads to a decrease in the demand for inferior goods.

- [ ] True
- [ ] False

---


## SOLUTION KEY

<details>
<summary>Click to reveal answers</summary>

#### Q1
- **Answer:** `B`
- **Explanation:** When the exchange rate depreciates, domestic goods become cheaper for foreign buyers, which typically increases the demand for domestic goods as they are more competitively priced in the international market.

#### Q2
- **Answer:** `True`
- **Explanation:** Inferior goods are those for which demand decreases as consumer income increases. This is a fundamental concept in economics related to the income effect on demand.
