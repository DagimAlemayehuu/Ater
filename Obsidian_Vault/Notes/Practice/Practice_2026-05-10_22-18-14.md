## THE CHALLENGE

### Q1 | MCQ
In a developing economy, what happens to the demand for domestic goods when a country's currency depreciates?

- [ ] **A)** It decreases due to increased imports
- [ ] **B)** It increases due to improved competitiveness
- [ ] **C)** It remains unchanged as depreciation only affects services
- [ ] **D)** It becomes inelastic to changes in price

---


## SOLUTION KEY

<details>
<summary>Click to reveal answers</summary>

#### Q1
- **Answer:** `B`
- **Explanation:** When a country's currency depreciates, its exports become cheaper for foreign buyers, and imports become more expensive for domestic consumers. This leads to an increase in the demand for domestic goods, as they become more competitive in both domestic and international markets.
