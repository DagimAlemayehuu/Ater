## 🔐 SOLUTION KEY (DO NOT PEEK)

<details>
<summary>Click to reveal answers and technical logic</summary>

#### Q1 Logic
- **Definitive Answer:** `A`
- **Mechanism:** When the supply of labor is perfectly elastic, an increase in the minimum wage leads to an increase in the quantity of labor supplied without changing the wage, as the supply curve is a horizontal line.

#### Q2 Logic
- **Definitive Answer:** `A`
- **Mechanism:** Imposing a tariff on imported machinery increases the domestic price of the machinery, making it more expensive for consumers and leading to a decrease in the quantity imported due to the higher price.

#### Q3 Logic
- **Definitive Answer:** `True`
- **Mechanism:** According to Keynesian economics, an increase in government spending on infrastructure increases aggregate demand, which can stimulate economic growth. As the economy grows, businesses may hire more workers to meet the increased demand for goods and services, leading to a decrease in the unemployment rate. This causal chain assumes that the economy is not already at full employment and that the increased demand translates into real economic activity.

#### Q4 Logic
- **Definitive Answer:** `False`
- **Mechanism:** While a depreciation of a country's currency can make its exports cheaper and more competitive on the global market, which could lead to an increase in exports, the outcome is not always straightforward. The J-curve effect suggests that initially, a depreciation might worsen the trade balance because the increase in export volume might not be enough to offset the decrease in export prices, and imports might become more expensive. Only in the long run, if the country's exports are elastic, might the trade balance improve.

#### Q5 Logic
- **Definitive Answer:** `When the government increases its spending on infrastructure projects but finances this increase through higher taxes, the net effect on aggregate demand is complex. The increased government spending would typically shift the aggregate demand curve to the right, as it directly adds to the demand for goods and services. However, the increase in taxes would reduce disposable income for households and businesses, likely leading to a decrease in consumption and investment. This could offset some of the increase in government spending, potentially leaving the aggregate demand curve to shift to the right but by a lesser extent than if the spending increase were financed through borrowing or other non-tax means. The overall level of economic activity, as measured by GDP, would likely increase but at a slower pace due to the dampening effect of higher taxes on private sector demand.`
- **Mechanism:** This question requires the application of fiscal policy principles to a specific scenario. It tests the understanding of how changes in government spending and taxation affect aggregate demand and economic activity. The correct answer demonstrates an understanding that while increased government spending can stimulate economic activity by shifting the aggregate demand curve to the right, the method of financing this spending (in this case, through increased taxes) can influence the magnitude of the effect. The explanation provided illustrates the causal tracing of such a policy change, considering both the direct effects of increased government spending and the indirect effects of higher taxes on private sector behavior.

#### Q6 Logic
- **Definitive Answer:** `left`
- **Mechanism:** When the government imposes a tax on the production of a good, it increases the cost of production. This causes the supply curve to shift left, as producers are willing to supply less of the good at each price level. As a result, the equilibrium price of the good increases, and the equilibrium quantity decreases.

#### Q7 Logic
- **Definitive Answer:** `right`
- **Mechanism:** When the demand curve shifts right, and the supply curve also shifts right, the equilibrium quantity of the good increases. The effect on the equilibrium price depends on the relative magnitudes of the shifts, but the quantity definitely increases.

#### Q8 Logic
- **Definitive Answer:** `None`
- **Mechanism:** When the central bank increases the reserve requirement ratio, banks have to keep a larger portion of their deposits in reserve and lend less. This reduction in lending leads to a decrease in the money supply. With less money circulating, interest rates tend to increase due to the higher demand for loans. Therefore, the correct match is 'Interest Rates' with 'Increase' and 'Money Supply' with 'Decrease'.

#### Q9 Logic
- **Definitive Answer:** `The country's exports decrease because foreign buyers find the products more expensive due to the appreciated currency., The decrease in exports leads to a trade deficit, or a widening of a trade surplus if one existed previously., Domestic production in export-oriented industries decreases due to reduced demand from foreign markets.`
- **Mechanism:** When a country's currency appreciates, its exports become more expensive for foreign buyers. This typically leads to a decrease in the quantity of exports sold. A decrease in exports can lead to a trade deficit if imports exceed exports or can widen a trade surplus if one previously existed but is now smaller. As exports decrease, domestic production in industries that produce goods for export also decreases due to reduced demand from foreign markets. Therefore, the correct sequence reflects these causal relationships.

#### Q10 Logic
- **Definitive Answer:** `The demand for domestic smartphones increases.`
- **Mechanism:** When the government increases the tax on imported electronics, it leads to a rise in their prices, making them more expensive for consumers. Assuming domestic smartphones have a similar functionality and remain unchanged in price, consumers are likely to shift their demand towards the domestic smartphones due to their relatively lower price. This is an example of a substitution effect, where consumers substitute one good for another due to a change in relative prices. Therefore, the demand for domestic smartphones increases.

#### Q11 Logic
- **Definitive Answer:** `$5 million`
- **Mechanism:** The change in tariff revenue is the difference between the new tariff revenue and the initial tariff revenue, which is $10 million - $5 million = $5 million.
