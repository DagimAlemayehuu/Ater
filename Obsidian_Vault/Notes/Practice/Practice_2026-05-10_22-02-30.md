## THE CHALLENGE

### Q1 | MCQ
In a developing economy, if the government increases its spending on infrastructure, what happens to the aggregate demand and the country's economic growth rate?

- [ ] **A)** Aggregate demand decreases, and economic growth rate slows down
- [ ] **B)** Aggregate demand increases, but economic growth rate remains unchanged
- [ ] **C)** Aggregate demand increases, and economic growth rate accelerates
- [ ] **D)** Aggregate demand remains unchanged, but economic growth rate accelerates

---

### Q2 | MATCHING
In the context of Central Banking & Monetary Policy, match the following terms with their definitions:

| Concept | Match |
| :--- | :--- |
| Open Market Operations | __________ |
| Monetary Policy | __________ |
| Fiscal Policy | __________ |
| Inflation Rate | __________ |

**Options:** `The actions of a central bank to control the money supply and interest rates to promote economic growth and stability`, `The buying and selling of government securities by a central bank to control the money supply and interest rates`, `A measure of the rate of change in prices of a basket of goods and services over time`, `The use of government spending and taxation to influence the overall level of economic activity`

---


## SOLUTION KEY

<details>
<summary>Click to reveal answers</summary>

#### Q1
- **Answer:** `C`
- **Explanation:** When the government increases its spending on infrastructure, it directly injects more money into the economy, which increases aggregate demand. This increase in aggregate demand can lead to higher production levels, more employment, and ultimately, an acceleration in the country's economic growth rate.

#### Q2
- **Answer:** `None`
- **Explanation:** Understanding the core principles of Central Banking & Monetary Policy, including tools and concepts used to manage the economy.

</details>
