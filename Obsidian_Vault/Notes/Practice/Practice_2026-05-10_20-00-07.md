## 🎯 THE CHALLENGE

### Q1 | ORDER
In the context of International Trade Analysis, if a country's currency appreciates, causing its exports to become more expensive for foreign buyers, what is the likely sequence of events regarding its trade balance and domestic production?

- [ ] The country's exports decrease because foreign buyers find the products more expensive due to the appreciated currency.
- [ ] The decrease in exports leads to a trade deficit, or a widening of a trade surplus if the country was previously experiencing one.
- [ ] Domestic production in export-oriented industries may decrease due to reduced demand from foreign markets.

---

#### Q1 Logic
- **Definitive Answer:** `Correct Step 1, Correct Step 2, Correct Step 3`
- **Mechanism:** When a country's currency appreciates, it makes the country's exports more expensive for foreign buyers. This typically leads to a decrease in exports (Step 1). The decrease in exports can result in a trade deficit if the country was not previously in a surplus position, or it can lead to a widening of a trade surplus if the country was previously experiencing one (Step 2). As exports decrease, domestic production in industries that are heavily export-oriented may also decrease due to reduced demand from foreign markets (Step 3).
