#### Q1 Logic
- **Definitive Answer:** `B`
- **Mechanism:** An increase in government spending on infrastructure leads to an increase in aggregate demand, as the government's expenditure injects more money into the economy. This increase in aggregate demand can stimulate economic growth, leading to an acceleration of the country's economic growth rate.

#### Q2 Logic
- **Definitive Answer:** `True`
- **Mechanism:** According to the principles of macroeconomics, an increase in government spending financed by higher taxes represents an increase in government expenditure (G) which is a component of aggregate demand (AD). However, the increase in taxes will reduce disposable income, and thus consumption (C), another component of AD. The net effect on aggregate demand depends on the marginal propensity to consume (MPC) and the multiplier effect. However, in a simple analysis assuming the MPC is less than 1, and considering the direct effect, the increase in G will increase AD, while the increase in taxes will decrease AD. If the increase in G equals the decrease in C (induced by higher taxes), then AD could remain unchanged. This scenario assumes a specific condition where the tax increase offsets the government spending increase in terms of net effect on AD, which can be considered theoretically possible under certain conditions, making the statement potentially true under specific assumptions.

#### Q3 Logic
- **Definitive Answer:** ``
- **Mechanism:** When the government increases its spending on infrastructure projects, it directly injects more money into the economy. This increase in government spending shifts the aggregate demand (AD) curve to the right. As the AD curve shifts to the right, it intersects the short-run aggregate supply (SRAS) curve at a higher level of real GDP and a higher price level. 

The increase in government spending has a ripple effect:
1. **Consumption**: As government spending increases, it creates more jobs and increases income for workers involved in infrastructure projects. This rise in income boosts consumption because workers have more disposable income to spend on goods and services.

2. **Investment**: The increase in economic activity and the prospect of higher returns on investment due to increased demand for goods and services can encourage businesses to invest more in capital projects. This can include expanding production capacity, improving technology, or enhancing product lines.

3. **GDP**: The overall effect on GDP is an increase. The initial injection of government spending adds directly to GDP. The subsequent increases in consumption and investment add further to GDP. According to the multiplier effect, the initial injection of spending can lead to a multiplied increase in GDP because each round of spending generates additional income and consumption.

In conclusion, an increase in government spending on infrastructure projects can stimulate economic activity by increasing aggregate demand, which in turn can lead to higher levels of consumption, investment, and overall GDP.

#### Q4 Logic
- **Definitive Answer:** `rise`
- **Mechanism:** When demand increases while supply remains constant, the market equilibrium shifts, leading to a higher price. This is a fundamental principle in economics, illustrating the law of supply and demand.

#### Q5 Logic
- **Definitive Answer:** `{'Increase in Inflation Rate': 'Tightening of Monetary Policy', 'Decrease in Unemployment Rate': 'Tightening of Monetary Policy', 'Increase in GDP Growth Rate': 'Tightening of Monetary Policy'}`
- **Mechanism:** When the economy experiences high inflation, central banks typically respond by increasing the policy rate to tighten monetary policy, thereby reducing demand and curbing inflationary pressures. An increase in inflation rate, a decrease in unemployment rate (which can lead to wage inflation), and an increase in GDP growth rate (which can lead to demand-pull inflation) all suggest that the economy is growing strongly and inflation is a concern, necessitating a tightening of monetary policy.

#### Q6 Logic
- **Definitive Answer:** `2`
- **Mechanism:** When a country's tariffs on imported electronics decrease, it becomes cheaper for consumers and businesses to import electronics. This typically leads to an increase in imports. As imports increase, domestic producers may face more competition, potentially leading to a decrease in domestic production of electronics. An increase in imports, assuming exports remain constant or do not increase by as much, usually causes the trade balance to worsen because more value is leaving the country. Therefore, the correct sequence is: Increase in imports, decrease in domestic production, and the trade balance worsens.

#### Q7 Logic
- **Definitive Answer:** `Increase by $500 million`
- **Mechanism:** The government spending multiplier is calculated as 1 / (1 - MPC). Given MPC = 0.8, the multiplier = 1 / (1 - 0.8) = 1 / 0.2 = 5. An increase in government spending by $100 million will lead to a total change in real GDP of $100 million * 5 = $500 million.

#### Q8 Logic
- **Definitive Answer:** `The trade balance worsens.`
- **Mechanism:** When a country's currency appreciates, its exports become more expensive for foreign buyers. If the demand for its exports is highly elastic, a small increase in price leads to a large decrease in the quantity demanded. As a result, the total revenue from exports decreases significantly. Since the country's exports decrease more substantially than its imports (assuming the demand for imports is less elastic or not as significantly affected), its trade balance worsens.

#### Q9 Logic
- **Definitive Answer:** `$5 million`
- **Mechanism:** The initial tariff revenue collected is 5% of $100 million, which is $5 million. After increasing the tariff to 10%, the new tariff revenue collected is 10% of $100 million, which is $10 million. The change in tariff revenue is $10 million - $5 million = $5 million.
