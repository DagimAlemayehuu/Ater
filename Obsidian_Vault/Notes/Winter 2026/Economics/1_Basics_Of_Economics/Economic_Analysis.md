## Mental Model

Economic analysis is like studying how a big airline company decides to use its resources, such as planes, fuel, and staff, to fly to different destinations. The company has limited resources, but it wants to satisfy the unlimited demand of passengers who want to travel to various places. Economic analysis helps the company make choices about how to use its resources efficiently to meet the demand.

## How the Economics Actually Work

Economic analysis is a process of evaluating the outcomes of economic choices in terms of criteria such as efficiency, equity, and stability. 
- WHAT: It involves studying how individuals, firms, and governments make choices about how to use scarce resources to satisfy unlimited wants. 
- WHY: Economic analysis exists because resources are scarce, and people have unlimited wants, leading to the need for efficient allocation of resources. 
- HOW: The mechanism of economic analysis involves:
  1. Identifying the scarce resources and unlimited wants.
  2. Evaluating the different choices and their outcomes.
  3. Selecting the most efficient option based on criteria such as efficiency, equity, and stability.
  This process is closely related to [[Scarcity]], [[Unlimited_Wants]], and [[Basic_Economic_Questions]]. 
  Economic analysis also relies on understanding [[Economic_Systems]], such as market economies, and the role of [[Decision_Making_Units]], like households and firms.

## The Formal Math & Models

The formal definition of economics, as stated in the source text, is: "Economics is a social science which studies about efficient allocation of scarce resources so as to attain the maximum fulfillment of unlimited human wants." This definition highlights the core focus of economic analysis on efficient resource allocation. The source text also mentions that economists study how choices are made in various settings and evaluate outcomes based on criteria such as efficiency, equity, and stability.
