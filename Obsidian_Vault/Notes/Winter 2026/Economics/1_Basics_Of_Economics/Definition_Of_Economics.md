## 2. How the Economics Actually Work

- WHAT: Economics is a social science that studies how to efficiently allocate scarce resources to attain the maximum fulfillment of unlimited human wants.
- WHY: This concept exists because people's wants are unlimited, but the resources available to satisfy those wants are scarce. This creates a fundamental problem that economics tries to solve. 
- HOW: 
  1. Resources are limited, but people's desires are not.
  2. Because of this, we have to make choices about how to use the resources we have.
  3. Economics helps us understand how to make those choices in a way that makes the most sense.
  4. It looks at how resources are allocated, how people make decisions, and how those decisions affect the community.
  Connections: This concept is closely related to [[Scarcity]], [[Human_Wants]], and [[Resource_Allocation]]. Understanding economics also involves looking at [[Basic_Economic_Questions]] and how different societies answer them.

## 3. The Formal Math & Models

The formal definition of economics, as provided, is: "Economics is a social science which studies about efficient allocation of scarce resources so as to attain the maximum fulfillment of unlimited human wants." ## 6. Common Misconceptions

- Some students might think economics is only about money or business, but it's actually about how people make choices with the resources they have.
- Others might believe that economics is just about being wealthy, but it's really about making the most of what we have to satisfy our wants and needs.
- A common misconception is that economics is not relevant to everyday life, but the principles of economics apply to all decisions that involve making choices about resources.
