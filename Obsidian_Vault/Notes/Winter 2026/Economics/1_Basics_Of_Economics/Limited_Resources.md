## 1. Mental Model

Imagine a small island with a limited amount of land, water, and natural resources. The islanders must decide how to allocate these resources to meet their needs and wants. They must choose what goods and services to produce, how to produce them, and for whom. This scenario illustrates the fundamental problem of [[Scarcity]] and the need for resource allocation. The islanders' decisions will be influenced by their [[Unlimited_Wants]] and the [[Basic_Economic_Questions]] they face.

## 2. Foundational Concept

The concept of [[Limited_Resources]] is a fundamental principle in economics. It states that economic resources, such as land, labor, and capital, are finite and cannot be replenished. This leads to the problem of [[Scarcity]], which requires individuals and societies to make choices about how to allocate resources. The basic questions of resource allocation are what, how, and for whom. These questions are central to the study of [[Macroeconomics]] and [[Positive_Economics]]. The concept of Limited Resources is closely related to Efficiency and Market Failure.

### Key Takeaways:

- Resources are limited or scarce.
- The basic questions of resource allocation are what, how, and for whom.
- Economic resources are limited, which leads to the fundamental problem of Scarcity.

## 3. Limitations & Edge Cases

The concept of limited resources assumes that resources are finite and cannot be replenished. A limitation of this concept is that it does not account for potential technological advancements that could increase resource availability. For example, the discovery of new extraction methods could increase the availability of natural resources. Additionally, the concept assumes that resources are evenly distributed, which may not be the case in reality. For instance, some regions may have an abundance of certain resources while others may have scarcity. Furthermore, the concept does not consider the impact of human behavior on resource allocation, such as the effects of [[Unlimited_Wants]] and [[Basic_Economic_Questions]] on resource utilization.
