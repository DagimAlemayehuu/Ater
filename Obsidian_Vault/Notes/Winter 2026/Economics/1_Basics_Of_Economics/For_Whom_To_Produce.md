## 5. Where It Breaks (Edge Cases & Flaws)

* **Insufficient Resources**: If resources are insufficient to meet all wants, the economy cannot produce enough to satisfy everyone, making distribution decisions challenging.
* **Undefined Preferences**: If consumer preferences are not clearly defined, it is difficult to determine how to distribute goods and services, making it hard to decide "for whom to produce".
* **Inefficient Allocation**: If resources are not allocated efficiently, the economy may not produce the optimal combination of goods and services, leading to suboptimal distribution outcomes.
