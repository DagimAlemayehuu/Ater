The problem of For Whom To Produce is a fundamental question in economics that relates to how a material product is to be distributed among the members of a society. The distribution of national product is a critical aspect of [[Macroeconomics]], as it deals with the allocation of resources and the resulting [[Efficiency]]. The problem of For Whom To Produce is closely related to [[Normative_Economics]], as it involves value judgments about who should receive the goods and services produced in an economy.
