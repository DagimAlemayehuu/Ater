## 1. Mental Model

A manufacturing firm, producing widgets, implements a new robotic assembly line that increases its production capacity by 30%. As a result, the firm can now supply 100 more widgets per month at the same price. This change allows the firm to meet growing demand and expand its market share. The firm's suppliers also benefit from the increased demand for raw materials.

## 2. Causal Mechanism

| Technological advancement enables a firm to produce and supply more in the market. This can be measured by the price elasticity of supply, which is calculated as $E_S = \frac{\Delta Q}{Q} \div \frac{\Delta P}{P} = \frac{\Delta Q}{\Delta P} * \frac{P}{Q}$. Alternatively, the arc elasticity of supply can be used: $E_S^A = \frac{Q_1 - Q_0}{Q_0 + Q_1} \div \frac{P_1 - P_0}{P_0 + P_1} = \frac{\Delta Q}{\Delta P} * \frac{P_0 + P_1}{Q_0 + Q_1}$. An increase in technological advancement can lead to an increase in the elasticity of supply, making the supply curve more Law Of Supply|elastic. This concept is closely related to [[Determinants_Of_Elasticity_Of_Supply]], which affect the responsiveness of suppliers to changes in market conditions. |

### Key Takeaways:

- A technological advancement can increase the price elasticity of supply by allowing firms to quickly respond to changes in market conditions.
- The arc elasticity of supply formula is particularly useful when analyzing the impact of technological changes on supply over time.
- Understanding technological advancement is crucial for analyzing [[Market_Equilibrium]], as it can shift the supply curve and affect market prices.

## 3. Limitations & Edge Cases

However, there are limitations to consider. For instance, the implementation of new technology can be costly, and the benefits may not be immediate. Additionally, technological advancements can lead to job displacement if not managed properly. Furthermore, the assumption that technological advancement always leads to an increase in supply may not hold in cases where the new technology is used to improve product quality rather than quantity. The impact of technological advancement on supply can also be influenced by factors such as [[Time_And_Eligibility]] and government policies.

## 5. Walkthrough

**Step 1:** The manufacturing firm initially produces 200 widgets at a price of $10.

**Step 2:** After implementing the new robotic assembly line, the firm increases production to 300 widgets without changing the price.

**Step 3:** To calculate the elasticity of supply, we first determine the changes in quantity and price: $\Delta Q = 300 - 200 = 100$, $\Delta P = 10 - 10 = 0$.

**Step 4:** However, since $\Delta P = 0$, the elasticity of supply $E_S$ is undefined using the initial formula.

**Step 5:** Instead, we recognize that the technological advancement makes the supply curve more elastic, allowing the firm to respond more readily to price changes.

**Step 6:** The arc elasticity of supply formula can be applied for a more accurate analysis: $E_S^A = rac{Q_1 - Q_0}{Q_0 + Q_1} \div rac{P_1 - P_0}{P_0 + P_1}$.

**Step 7:** Substituting the given values yields $E_S^A = rac{300 - 200}{200 + 300} \div rac{10 - 10}{10 + 10}$.

**Step 8:** Although $\Delta P = 0$ presents a challenge, we acknowledge that the elasticity increases significantly due to technological advancement.
