## 1. Mental Model

The manager of a popular coffee shop, "The Daily Grind," is considering raising the price of its best-selling latte from $4 to $5. Currently, 500 lattes are sold daily, and the shop's total revenue from lattes is $2,000. If the price increase leads to a drop in sales to 300 lattes, the manager must understand how the price change affects total revenue. This scenario illustrates the concept of elasticity and its impact on total revenue.

## 2. Causal Mechanism

The effect of a price change on total revenue can be understood through the concept of Price Elasticity Of Demand, which measures how responsive the quantity demanded of a good is to a change in its price. When demand is elastic, a price increase leads to a proportionally larger decrease in quantity demanded, resulting in lower total revenue. Conversely, when demand is inelastic, a price increase leads to a proportionally smaller decrease in quantity demanded, resulting in higher total revenue. The [[Arc_Elasticity_Of_Demand]] formula can be used to calculate elasticity over a range of prices. For example, if the price of the latte increases from $4 to $5 and the quantity demanded decreases from 500 to 300, the arc elasticity can be calculated to determine the elasticity of demand.

### Key Takeaways:

- A price increase will lead to a decrease in total revenue if demand is elastic.
- The [[Arc_Elasticity_Of_Demand]] formula provides a way to calculate elasticity over a range of prices.
- Understanding elasticity is crucial for businesses, like "The Daily Grind," to predict how price changes will affect their total revenue.

## 3. Limitations & Edge Cases

The concept of elasticity and total revenue assumes that other factors affecting demand, such as consumer income and prices of [[Substitute_Goods]], remain constant. In reality, these factors can change, influencing demand elasticity and the impact of price changes on total revenue. Additionally, the concept of elasticity may not apply uniformly across different time periods, as [[Time_And_Eligibility]] can affect demand responsiveness. Furthermore, businesses may face limitations in adjusting prices due to market competition or contractual obligations.

## 5. Walkthrough

**Step 1:** The initial price of the latte is $4 and the quantity sold is 500.

**Step 2:** After the price increase to $5, the quantity sold decreases to 300.

**Step 3:** The initial total revenue is $2000 and the new total revenue is $1500.

**Step 4:** The arc elasticity of demand is calculated to be -2.25.

**Step 5:** Since the absolute value of the arc elasticity is 2.25, the demand is elastic.

**Step 6:** When demand is elastic, a price increase leads to a elastic change in total revenue.
