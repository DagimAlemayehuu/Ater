# 1. Mental Model

The concept of Impact of Disability on Daily Life can be likened to a ripple effect in a pond, where the disability is the stone thrown into the water, creating waves that disturb the surface. Just as the ripples affect the surrounding water, the impact of disability affects various aspects of an individual's life, such as their [[Current_Life_Circumstances]] and [[Support_System]]. The size and intensity of the ripples can be influenced by factors like the individual's [[Personality_Disposition]] and [[Coping_Patterns]].

# 2. Requirement Elicitation & Specification

The impact of disability on daily life is a complex issue that involves understanding the individual's [[Nature_Of_Impairment]] and its effects on their daily activities. The [[Meaning_Of_Impairment_To_Individual]] and their [[Self_Efficacy_Beliefs]] play a crucial role in determining their response to the disability. Additionally, factors such as [[Economic_Factors]], [[Psychological_Factors]], and [[Family_And_Disability]] can influence the individual's experience of disability. A comprehensive approach to addressing the impact of disability on daily life must consider the individual's [[Special_Needs_Of_Pwds]] and provide [[Disability_Inclusive_Intervention]] strategies that promote [[Inclusive_Job_Opportunities]] and access to [[Assistive_Technology]].
