# 1. Mental Model

The concept of "Meaning of Impairment to Individual" can be likened to a river's impact on its surrounding landscape. Just as a river's flow can shape the landscape by carving out paths and creating unique geological features, an individual's impairment can shape their self-perception and identity by influencing their experiences, interactions, and personal growth. The river's course represents the individual's life journey, with the impairment being a significant geological event that alters the landscape of their self-concept.

# 2. Requirement Elicitation & Specification

The process of understanding the meaning of impairment to an individual involves considering various factors, including their [[Current_Life_Circumstances]], [[Support_System]], and [[Personality_Disposition]]. It is also essential to examine how the individual perceives their impairment, taking into account their [[Meaning_Of_Impairment_To_Individual]] and [[Self_Efficacy_Beliefs]]. Additionally, the impact of societal [[Economic_Factors]] and [[Psychological_Factors]] on the individual's self-perception must be assessed. The individual's [[Coping_Patterns]] and [[Rehabilitation_Team_Members]] can also play a crucial role in shaping their understanding of their impairment. By analyzing these factors, a comprehensive understanding of the meaning of impairment to the individual can be developed, ultimately informing [[Disability_Inclusive_Intervention]] strategies.

# 3. Ambiguity Failures & Scope Creep

Boundary conditions, such as significant life changes or unexpected events, can lead to ambiguity failures in understanding the meaning of impairment to an individual. For instance, if an individual's impairment is suddenly exacerbated, their self-perception may shift, leading to scope creep in their rehabilitation plan. Failure states, such as depression or anxiety, can also arise if the individual's impairment is not adequately addressed. The table below illustrates potential failure states and their causes.

| Failure State | Cause |
|---|---|
| Depression | Lack of [[Support_System]] or ineffective [[Coping_Patterns]] |
| Anxiety | Uncertainty about [[Current_Life_Circumstances]] or [[Economic_Factors]] |
| Scope Creep | Insufficient consideration of [[Psychological_Factors]] or [[Personality_Disposition]] |
