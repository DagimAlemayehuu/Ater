# 1. Mental Model

The Community Based Rehabilitation (CBR) program can be likened to a network of interconnected roads that facilitate access to various essential services for individuals with disabilities. Just as a well-planned road network enables smooth traffic flow and connectivity between cities, CBR provides a multi-dimensional home-based rehabilitation framework that links individuals with disabilities to public services such as education, self-employment, and healthcare. The CBR team, comprising various stakeholders, acts as traffic coordinators, ensuring that individuals with disabilities navigate these services effectively.

# 2. Systemic Function & Homeostasis

Community Based Rehabilitation [[Community_Based_Rehabilitation]] functions as a strategy to address the needs of individuals with disabilities in developing countries, taking into account their [[Current_Life_Circumstances]] and [[Support_System]]. The CBR approach aims to promote [[Inclusive_Job_Opportunities]] and provide access to essential services such as education, healthcare, and self-employment, thereby enhancing the individual's [[Self_Efficacy_Beliefs]] and [[Coping_Patterns]]. The [[Rehabilitation_Team_Members]] work collaboratively to provide multi-dimensional home-based rehabilitation services, considering the individual's [[Personality_Disposition]] and [[Meaning_Of_Impairment_To_Individual]]. Effective CBR implementation also relies on [[Assistive_Technology]] and [[Disability_Inclusive_Intervention]] to address [[Barriers_To_Employment]] and promote [[Prevention]]. By adopting a [[Twin_Track_Approach]], CBR fosters a supportive environment that acknowledges the [[Impact_Of_Disability_On_Daily_Life]] and facilitates [[Special_Needs_Of_Pwds]].

# 3. Pathological Failure & Necrosis

Failure of the Community Based Rehabilitation program can occur when there is a lack of coordination among stakeholders, resulting in inadequate access to essential services for individuals with disabilities. This can lead to a breakdown in the rehabilitation process, causing individuals to become isolated and excluded from community activities. The CBR program can also fail when it does not take into account the individual's [[Economic_Factors]] and [[Psychological_Factors]], leading to a mismatch between the services provided and the individual's needs. In extreme cases, the program's failure can result in increased [[Family_And_Disability]] stress and decreased [[Self_Efficacy_Beliefs]], ultimately hindering the individual's ability to cope with their impairment.
