# 3. Ambiguity Failures & Scope Creep

Boundary conditions, such as changes in the individual's circumstances or the availability of resources, can impact the special needs of PwDs and lead to ambiguity failures. If the scope of support services is not clearly defined, it can lead to scope creep, where the needs of PwDs are not adequately addressed. Failure states, such as inadequate support or inaccessible resources, can occur when the special needs of PwDs are not properly understood or addressed. 

| Failure State | Description |
|---|---|
| Inadequate Support | Insufficient resources or services to meet the special needs of PwDs |
| Inaccessible Resources | Resources or services that are not accessible or usable by PwDs | 
| Scope Creep | Expansion of support services beyond the defined scope, leading to inadequate addressing of special needs | 
| Ambiguity Failure | Unclear or ambiguous definition of special needs, leading to inadequate support or resources |
