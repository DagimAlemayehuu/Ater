# 1. Mental Model

The concept of Current Life Circumstances can be mapped to a river's flow, where the river represents the individual's life journey and the rocks or obstacles in the river represent the challenges or factors that influence their current situation. Just as the river's flow can be affected by the shape of its banks, the individual's life journey is shaped by their Current Life Circumstances, such as their independence or dependence on others. The river's depth and speed can also be influenced by external factors, such as rainfall or seasonal changes, which can be likened to the [[Economic_Factors]] and [[Political_Factors]] that impact an individual's life.

# 2. Requirement Elicitation & Specification

The assessment of an individual's Current Life Circumstances involves understanding their [[Support_System]], including their family and caregivers, and how it impacts their daily life, particularly in relation to their [[Impact_Of_Disability_On_Daily_Life]]. Their [[Current_Life_Circumstances]] also influence their [[Self_Efficacy_Beliefs]] and [[Coping_Patterns]], which in turn affect their ability to navigate their situation. Furthermore, their [[Personality_Disposition]] and [[Individuals_Personality]] play a significant role in shaping their experiences and interactions with their environment. The [[Rehabilitation_Team_Members]] must consider these factors when developing a [[Disability_Inclusive_Intervention]] plan. Additionally, the individual's [[Special_Needs_Of_Pwds]] must be taken into account to ensure that the support provided is tailored to their unique requirements.

# 3. Ambiguity Failures & Scope Creep

Boundary conditions, such as a sudden change in [[Economic_Factors]] or a shift in [[Current_Life_Circumstances]], can lead to failure states, including increased stress or decreased well-being. If the individual's needs are not properly assessed, or if there is a lack of coordination among [[Rehabilitation_Team_Members]], the support provided may not effectively address their [[Special_Needs_Of_Pwds]]. 

| Failure State | Description |
|---|---|
| Increased Stress | Sudden change in economic factors |
| Decreased Well-being | Lack of coordination among rehabilitation team members |
| Ineffective Support | Failure to assess individual's needs | 

In such cases, the scope of the support system may need to be revisited to ensure that it aligns with the individual's evolving [[Current_Life_Circumstances]].
