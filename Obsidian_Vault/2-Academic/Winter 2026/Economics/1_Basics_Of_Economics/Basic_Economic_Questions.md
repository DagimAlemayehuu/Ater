# 1. Mental Model

Imagine you have a lemonade stand and can only make a certain amount of lemonade each day. You need to decide how much lemonade to make, how to make it (like choosing between a fancy or simple recipe), and who to sell it to (like friends, family, or people walking by). These are basic questions that any business or economy needs to answer.

# 2. Economic Theory

The Basic Economic Questions refer to the fundamental decisions that economies must make regarding the allocation of [[Economic_Resources]]. These questions are: What to produce ([[What_To_Produce]]), How to produce it ([[How_To_Produce]]), and For whom to produce it ([[For_Whom_To_Produce]]). These questions arise due to [[Scarcity]] and [[Unlimited_Human_Wants]], requiring [[Decision_Making_Units]] to make choices.

## 4. Walkthrough

* The economy decides **What to Produce** (e.g., lemonade) based on consumer demand and resource availability.
* Next, it determines **How to Produce** (e.g., simple or fancy recipe) depending on available resources and technology.
* Then, it figures out **For Whom to Produce** (e.g., friends, family, or people walking by) based on target markets and consumer needs.
* Finally, the economy distributes goods and services based on production decisions.

## 5. Market Failures

This concept can fail when there are externalities, such as environmental degradation, or when markets are imperfect, leading to unequal distribution of resources. Additionally, the model assumes rational decision-making, which may not always be the case in real-world economies.
