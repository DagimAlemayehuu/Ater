## 1. Mental Model

Imagine a small coffee shop. The owner has to decide how much coffee to brew based on the number of customers expected. This is a simple example of [[Economic_Resources]] allocation. The owner wants to maximize profits, but is limited by the amount of coffee beans available and the number of customers willing to buy coffee. This scenario illustrates the concept of [[Scarcity]] and how it affects decision-making.

## 2. Foundational Concept

The Theory of Economics is a fundamental concept in understanding how societies function. It provides a framework for analyzing how individuals and firms make decisions about how to allocate [[Economic_Resources]] in the face of [[Scarcity]]. The theory is based on the idea that [[Human_Wants]] are unlimited, but the resources available to satisfy those wants are limited. This leads to the basic economic questions of what to produce, how to produce it, and for whom to produce it. The Theory of Economics also relies on the concept of [[Capitalist_Economy]], where individuals and firms make decisions based on their own self-interest. By understanding how these decisions are made, we can better understand the workings of a Capitalist Economy and the role of Positive Economics and Normative Economics in shaping economic outcomes.

### Key Takeaways:

- The Theory of Economics provides a simplified picture of reality.
- It helps understand how societies allocate Economic Resources.
- The theory is based on the concept of Scarcity.

## 3. Limitations & Edge Cases

One limitation of the Theory of Economics is that it assumes [[Human_Wants]] are unlimited, which may not always be the case in reality. Another limitation is that it often relies on the assumption of [[Deductive_Reasoning]], which may not account for real-world complexities and uncertainties. Additionally, the theory may not fully capture the impact of [[Inductive_Reasoning]] on economic decision-making. The theory also assumes that individuals and firms make rational decisions, which may not always be the case due to factors like Irrationality.
