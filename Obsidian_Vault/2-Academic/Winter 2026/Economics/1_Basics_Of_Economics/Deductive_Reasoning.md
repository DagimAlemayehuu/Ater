## 3. Limitations & Edge Cases

The deductive reasoning approach is limited by its reliance on the accuracy of the initial general statement or premise, as a flawed foundation can lead to erroneous conclusions. Moreover, deductive reasoning struggles with handling ambiguity and uncertainty, often present in real-world scenarios, as it requires clear and precise definitions to derive valid conclusions. Additionally, deductive reasoning can be overly rigid, failing to account for exceptions or nuances, and may not be effective in situations where the relationships between variables are complex or nonlinear, ultimately constraining its ability to capture the intricacies of economic phenomena, such as consumer behavior or market dynamics.
