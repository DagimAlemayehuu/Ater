### Q1 [mcq]: What is the primary purpose of an Entity-Relationship (ER) diagram in database design?

- **B)** To model the relationships between entities
- **C)** To specify the data types of attributes
- **D)** To optimize database performance

***

### Q2 [mcq]: Which of the following is a characteristic of a weak entity type?

- **B)** It exists independently
- **C)** Its primary key is derived from another entity
- **D)** It has no attributes

***

### Q3 [mcq]: What is the term for a relationship between two or more entities of the same type?
- **A)** One-to-one
- **B)** One-to-many
- **C)** Many-to-many
- **D)** Recursive

***

- **B)** Foreign key
- **C)** Cardinality
- **D)** Normalization

***

- **C)** Composite key
