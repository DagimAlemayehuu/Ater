### Q5 [matching]: Match terms.
- Homogeneous Recurrence Relation  <-->  A sequence defined as $a_n = c_1a_{n-1} + c_2a_{n-2} + \cdots + c_ka_{n-k}$, where $c_1, c_2, \ldots, c_k$ are constants.
- Linear Homogeneous Recurrence Relation  <-->  A type of recurrence relation where the function f(n) = 0 for all n, depending only on previous terms.
- Characteristic Equation  <-->  A way to express a sequence, often used in the definition of a recurrence relation.
- Sequence Notation  <-->  An equation used to find a general formula for the terms of a linear homogeneous recurrence relation.
