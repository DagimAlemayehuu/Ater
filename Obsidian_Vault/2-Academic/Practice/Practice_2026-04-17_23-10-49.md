### Q1 [mcq]: What is the primary purpose of an Entity-Relationship (ER) diagram in database design?
- **A)** To define the database schema
- **B)** To specify the database management system
- **C)** To model the data structure and relationships
- **D)** To optimize database performance

***

### Q2 [mcq]: Which of the following is a characteristic of a strong entity type?
- **A)** It has a composite key
- **B)** It has a primary key that is not derived from another entity
- **C)** It is dependent on another entity for its existence
- **D)** It has a multi-valued attribute

***

### Q3 [mcq]: What is the purpose of mapping an ER schema to a set of relational tables?
- **A)** To improve data security
- **B)** To enhance data integrity
- **C)** To implement the database in an RDBMS
- **D)** To optimize query performance

***

### Q4 [mcq]: What is a weak entity type in the context of ER modeling?
- **A)** An entity with a composite key
- **B)** An entity that exists independently
- **C)** An entity that relies on another entity for its existence
- **D)** An entity with a multi-valued attribute

***

### Q5 [mcq]: What is the term for a relationship between two or more entities where one entity is dependent on the other?

- **B)** One-to-Many (1:N)
- **C)** Identifying Relationship
- **D)** Recursive Relationship
