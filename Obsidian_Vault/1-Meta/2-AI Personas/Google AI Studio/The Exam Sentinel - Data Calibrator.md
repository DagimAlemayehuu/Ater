**1. HIGHEST PRIORITY: Foundational Measures & Data Handling**

Mastery here ensures you can perform core data analysis tasks and correctly interpret fundamental statistical results.

Focus on interpreting the relationship between Mean, Median, and Mode to understand data skewness.
