**VI. Strategic Study Methodology**

To counteract identified growth areas such as low discipline and a tendency to give up when overwhelmed, and to reallocate time from passive note-taking to active understanding, the following three-step study workflow will be implemented. This system is designed to prioritize the cognitive struggle that forges true learning over the creation of superficial notes, directly leveraging the detailed course outlines.

*   **Step 1: In-Lecture (Capture Mode)**
    *   **Action:** Before class, quickly review the specific units/sub-units from the relevant course outline that will be covered. During lectures, create a messy, real-time mind map.
    *   **Goal:** The exclusive focus is on capturing the high-level structure and the connections between major ideas, guided by the outline, not on perfecting details or transcribing definitions. This method forces active listening and serves as a powerful memory trigger for the next step, preventing passive, low-value note-taking.
*   **Step 2: Synthesis Block (Struggle & Apply Mode)**
    *   **Action:** Schedule a non-negotiable "Synthesis Block" for each subject within 48 hours of the corresponding lecture.
    *   **Goal:** This is the core of the learning process where mental "weightlifting" occurs.
        1.  Using only the messy mind map from the lecture, and guided by the course outline's units and sub-units, attempt to write a full, detailed summary from memory. This active recall struggle, using the outline as a structured prompt, is the primary mechanism for building understanding.
        2.  After hitting the limit of recall for each unit, consult the textbook or other resources to fill in gaps and correct misunderstandings.
        3.  Immediately pivot from theory to application. Begin assignments, solve practice problems, and engage in coding and debugging, categorizing these efforts under the specific outline units they address.
*   **Step 3: Archiving (Consolidate Mode)**
    *   **Action:** To be performed only _after_ the Synthesis Block is complete and a genuine understanding has been achieved through struggle and application.
    *   **Goal:** Use the "Perfected Obsidian Summarizer v4.0 Ultra" to process the raw course materials (e.g., textbook chapters, lecture slides) into a clean, permanent, and interlinked knowledge asset. This step is not a substitute for learning but a final reward—the creation of a perfect, high-fidelity record of knowledge that has already been mastered, organized precisely according to the course's units and sub-units.
