# Life OS Vault Structure v3.1

This vault follows a static numbering system (0- to 6-) to ensure consistent organization and agent accessibility. ALL emoji usage is prohibited.

## 0-Meta
System files, templates, and agent configurations.
- `0-Templates`: Reusable note templates.
- `1-Scripts`: Automation scripts.
- `2-Config`: Life OS sync settings.

## 1-NotionMirror
Direct reflection of Notion databases. Managed by **Notion Librarian**.
- `Personal`: Tasks, Goals, Projects.
- `Finance`: Income, Expenses, Budgets.
- `Fitness`: Workouts, Measurements.

## 2-Academic
University and learning resources. Managed by **Scholar** and **OKA Sentinel**.
- `0-Year 1`
- `1-Year 2`
- `2-Year 3`
- `3-Year 4`
Structure: `Year > Semester > Course > Unit > Notes`

## 3-Research
Area-specific knowledge clusters.
- `0-Artificial Intelligence`
- `1-Computer Science`
- `2-Prompt Engineering`
- `3-Summary Archive`

## 4-Projects
Active and archived initiatives.
- `0-Active`: Current high-focus projects.
- `1-Archive`: Completed or paused projects.
- `2-Portfolio`: Showcased work.

## 5-Inbox
The triage zone for new information.
- `0-Raw`: Files waiting for classification.
- `1-Processing`: Currently being handled by agents.

## 6-Assets
Legacy PDF store and binary assets for knowledge notes.
- `0-Images`
- `1-PDFs`

---
**Note**: All folders are indexed by the **DevOps Guardian** for RAG-based intelligence.
